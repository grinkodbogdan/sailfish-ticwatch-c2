          Internet over Bluetooth

          The watch can use the phone's mobile data or WiFi through Bluetooth when it has no WiFi of its own. Android only lets you switch this on yourself, so it takes three steps once:

          1. Make the watch visibleOn the watch: Settings → Bluetooth → turn on “Visible to phones”.
          2. Pair it for internetPair the watch in the phone's Bluetooth settings (it appears as TicWatch C2). Confirm the code on both screens.
          3. Turn on Bluetooth tetheringIn Hotspot & tethering, switch on “Bluetooth tethering”.
          4. Use it on the watchOn the watch: Settings → Bluetooth → tap your phone → “Phone internet”. From then on the watch connects by itself whenever it has no WiFi.

          Some phones switch Bluetooth tethering off after a restart — turn it on again here if the watch loses internet.
